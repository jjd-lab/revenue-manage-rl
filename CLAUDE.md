# CLAUDE.md

Working notes for AI coding agents; `README.md` is the project's own front door.

Config-driven revenue management for a **fictional 10,000-seat amphitheater**:
a Gymnasium env over a 100-day booking window where the agent sets two levers per
day, **price** and **availability** (a selling limit). Pluggable demand models,
classical baselines, Stable-Baselines3 PPO/SAC (TD3 is wired but unused in the
published results), and a control layer that can hold the second lever instead
of the agent. `runs/` holds the findings and is tracked; `artifacts/` holds
trained weights, is **untracked raw material**, and must be retrained locally
(README § Model checkpoints). Headline comparison:
`runs/joint_vs_price_only_soft_aware/`.

## Where things are documented

- `wiki/index.md` — routes to everything; `wiki/SCHEMA.md` is the wiki contract
- `docs/` — reader-facing design, evaluation protocol, experiment narrative

## Rules

- **The venue, the demand corpus, and every number in `runs/` are synthetic**,
  generated by `demand/synthesize.py`. Keep the domain wording generic —
  "venue", "production-like", "your own CSV" — and keep it that way.
- **`env.capacity`, the price band, and the selling-limit bounds in
  `configs/default.yaml` are frozen.** Observations are normalized against them, so
  changing any one invalidates every checkpoint (the five §7 headline ones and
  the later retrains) and every table in `runs/`.
  Same for the reward-shaping weights. Retrain deliberately or not at all.
- The myopic baseline's overbook/keep constants (`baselines/policies.py`) are a
  weaker case, not a frozen scale: its selling limit never binds, so they move no
  published number — but they are the behaviour-cloning target, so changing them
  means `rprl-bc-sac` no longer reproduces the shipped checkpoint.
- **`runs/` is findings, `artifacts/` is raw material.** Never commit `artifacts/`
  or move checkpoints into `runs/`. Do not hand-edit generated files in `runs/`
  (`*.csv`, `*.png`, `comparison.md`, `soft_aware_*.md`) — rerun the `run_headline.py`
  / `final_eval.py` there. `NOTES.md` and `README.md` in `runs/` are hand-written.
  Every run uses held-out seeds 0–29.
- **Use `.venv` at the repo root** — `scripts/run_smoke.sh` sources it by path.
- `configs/default.yaml` and `src/reservation_pricing/configs/default.yaml` must
  stay byte-identical (a test enforces it).
- Do not lead with overall `remain > 1500` as a quality metric; soft-day undersell
  is structural under this demand model. Use the soft-aware scores instead
  (`docs/EVALUATING_POLICIES.md`).
- `ruff check .` and `pytest -m "not slow" -q` before every commit; the full
  suite before touching anything under `runs/`.
- After changes that alter behavior, architecture, or conventions, sync the wiki
  per `wiki/SCHEMA.md`.
